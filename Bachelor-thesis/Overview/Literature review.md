Critically analyses the existing research on topic

Helps to situate research within context of existing scholarship (allows to critically evaluate existing research)

Should address questions such as:
-  "What are the limitations of the existing research?"
-  "What gaps does the current research leave open?"
-  "How can my research contribute to the current body of scholarship?"
