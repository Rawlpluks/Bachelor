## Measure theory & probability
Foundational reading - Recommended to start early (especially [[Schilling]])

- [[Schilling]], R.L. - *Measures, Integrals and Martingales* (Cambridge). Flagged as a self-contained introduction at exactly the right level for the foundations chapter.
- [[Williams]], D. - *Probability with Martingales* (Cambridge). The standard reference connecting measure theory to probability

## Survival analysis
The bridge to the ReSurv counting-process machinery

- [[ABGK|Andersen, Borgan, Gill & Keiding]] - *Statistical Models Based on Counting Processes* (Springer). The authoritative measure-theoretic foundation. Heavy, but the "bible" for this material.
- [[Matinussen]], T. & Scheike, T.H. - *Dynamic Regression Models for Survival Data* (Springer)

Start with [[Schilling]] early and trat the measure theory as "applied" - understanding and using the tools rather than re-proving the fouindational theorems from scratch.
