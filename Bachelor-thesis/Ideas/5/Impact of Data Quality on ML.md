#### Impact of Data Quality on Machine Learning Investment Models

*How does the quality and variety of data used in machine learning models influence the success of investment strategies?*

Assess the extent to which data inputs (e.g. historical stock prices, macroeconomic data, alternative data) affect the model's performance. 
Could also explore how data scarcity or noisy data impacts predictions and investment outcomes.

- Read about [[Data Preprocessing Techniques]] in machine learning, particularly in the context of financial data
- Look into the role of [[Alternative Data]] in finance, such as satellite imagery, social media data, and more
- Read about [[Overfitting]] and [[Underfitting]] issues in financial modelling
